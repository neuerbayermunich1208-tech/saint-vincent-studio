    From idea to object

    Six steps. No shortcuts.

    A design is measured by how many times it was redrawn, not by how many days it took.

    1. Concept

      A question and a sketch. What the object is for, and why someone would want to keep it.

    2. Design

      Proportion, form, joinery. Every dimension is drawn before anything is made.

    3. Prototype

      The first physical model, to test what a drawing cannot tell you.

    4. Refine

      Tolerances corrected, proportions rebalanced, anything unnecessary removed.

    5. Manufacture

      Made in the studio, with a controlled process for each part.

    6. Finish

      Surfaces finished, each piece checked and packed by hand.
